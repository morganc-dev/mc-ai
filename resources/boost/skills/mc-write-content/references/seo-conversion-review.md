# SEO and Conversion Review

Evaluate an existing page without modifying it. Order findings by impact and give each finding a location, observation, consequence, and the smallest outcome that resolves it.

## Method

1. Open the page, read its structure, inspect it visually, and check the console. Read raw HTML when metadata or content without JavaScript must be verified.
2. Establish the goal and primary action from the product record or brief. Otherwise, state the goal the page appears to pursue.
3. Apply the SEO grid, conversion grid, and journey-friction pass.
4. Classify each point as blocking the goal, weakening it, or merely preferable.

## SEO Grid

- HTTP status, `title`, meta description, single `h1`, heading order, canonical, indexation, and slug.
- Alignment between intent, title, opening paragraph, and actual content.
- Incoming and outgoing internal links with descriptive anchors.
- Images: alt-text intent and clearly excessive weight.
- Structured data consistent with visible content.
- Essential content available without JavaScript when required.

## Conversion Grid

- The first viewport explains who the page is for, what it provides, and what to do next.
- The primary action is visible, singular, and named after its outcome.
- Each proof point sits near the claim it supports.
- Forms limit fields to the need, use visible labels, and explain errors and next steps.
- Steps, choices, jargon, distractions, and dead ends before the action are justified.
- Pricing or process, contact details, and legal information are findable; no deceptive pattern is present.
