# Product Record

Create or update a product record in the location declared by the project. Include only confirmed facts and explicitly open decisions. Omit an empty section instead of filling it artificially.

```markdown
# Product

## Platform

## Users

## Product Purpose

## Positioning

## Operating Context

## Capabilities and Constraints

## Brand Commitments

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

## Accessibility & Inclusion
```

Write the content in the working language. Keep section headings stable so tools and downstream work can reuse them.

## Tone of Voice

Define it under `Brand Commitments` from the audience's language and real commitments:

- register and level of formality, for example `tu` or `vous` in French;
- three adjectives, each illustrated by one fitting sentence and one counterexample;
- words to use and avoid;
- sentence length and structure;
- adjustments for errors, empty states, and legal copy.

Label inferences as such. Record missing evidence so later work cannot fabricate it. Never include real personal data without explicit permission.
