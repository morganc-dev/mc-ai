# Design Inputs

Extract what already exists before asking questions:

- audience, positioning, tone, and evidence from the product record or brief;
- real content from project documentation;
- supplied brand guidelines, logos, photos, screenshots, and references;
- palette, typography, spacing, iconography, and components from the existing interface;
- theme tokens, public assets, and font licenses from the repository.

## Inputs to Settle

- Creation, redesign, or refinement, and what must be preserved.
- Binding brand rules and remaining freedom.
- Logo formats and variants, especially SVG and monochrome.
- Binding colors and semantic roles, plus any dark scheme.
- Fonts licensed for web use and required character support.
- Available imagery, rights, and who supplies it.
- Two or three liked and disliked references, with reasons.
- Three qualities to evoke and three perceptions to avoid.
- Devices, usage contexts, slow networks, or other real constraints.
- Required accessibility level, with AA as the default for a public interface.

Ask once for gaps that genuinely change the direction. For each question, explain its consequence and the assumption used if unanswered. Mark missing assets as `[TO PROVIDE: expected format]`.
