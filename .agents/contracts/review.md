# Review Contract

The reviewer reports without modifying the audited target.

```text
Verdict: approved | changes_required | decision_required
Scope and baseline:
Findings:
Validated elements:
Checks performed:
Limitations and unverified items:
```

Every actionable finding includes:

```text
Severity: defect | risk | improvement
Location:
Evidence:
Consequence:
Minimum acceptable outcome:
```

A preference not tied to an explicit criterion or demonstrated consequence remains an optional improvement.
